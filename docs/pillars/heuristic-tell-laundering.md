# Heuristic: Tell Laundering

## Recognition

This heuristic fires when prose discipline fails through the register. Two moments:

1. **Draft-time fill.** A unit written from no position gets filled by familiar rhetorical machinery: slot filling, evidence dumps, register lexicon at density, position overwrite. The register can also overwrite a position that was already present.
2. **Repair-time laundering.** A flagged tell is answered by substitution: the letter of a check is satisfied while the work the check represents is avoided.

Laundering triggers:

1. **Post-correction substitution.** A reader flags a tell, and the proposed fix changes the symbol or phrase while preserving its rhetorical move. A dash becomes a semicolon or parenthetical. A summary label becomes a paraphrased summary label. A hedge becomes a different hedge. A padded triplet becomes a padded four-part list.
2. **Compliance performance.** A correction is answered with a self-assessment or repair narrative while the artifact remains unrepaired or barely changed.
3. **Self-review substitution.** Clearing a token checklist substitutes for re-anchoring the prose. Symbols are counted; structure and claims remain untouched.

Draft-time fill can occur before any visible correction or review. This heuristic owns both moments: the position tests below apply at draft time and at repair time.

Cues:

- A second edit keeps the sentence structure and changes one connective or punctuation mark.
- The rewrite is more clipped without the argument becoming tighter.
- One cluster of punctuation becomes another cluster with the same function.
- A flagged signpost is replaced by a synonymous signpost.
- The reply being drafted explains compliance instead of delivering the repair.
- A unit's sections exist because the template or scaffold has them; no position selected the content.
- A rewrite looks cleaner but a claim became weaker, vaguer, or ownerless without a stated reason.

## Move

### Step 0: Route the Flag

| Flag shape | Examples | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Symbol-level | "too many hyphens"; "that list is padded" | Apply Steps 1 to 3 to the flagged forms. |
| Register-level | "reads like a diary"; "generic model prose"; "tells everywhere" | Apply the register core to the whole unit: Position and Portability. Symbol edits cannot clear a register-level failure unless one overused lexeme was demonstrably the whole failure and the repaired unit passes both tests. |
| Scope-level | "too long"; "too detailed" | Keep only units whose positions the artifact owes. Prefer cutting optional units to thinning every sentence. |
| Requested meta-work | "audit this prose"; "explain the reasoning" | The analysis is the deliverable. Produce it rather than treating it as forbidden compliance narration. |

If a request could mean either repair or report, default to repair. If the same artifact is flagged again, route at least one level higher; repeated symbol-level repairs often reveal a register-level problem.

### Register Core

The register-level failure is prose filled from no position or overwritten by the register. Apply two tests to the whole unit:

| Test | Question | A failing answer means |
|---|---|---|
| Position | What does this unit assert, and could it be wrong? | Nothing checkable. Form the position, obtain the missing input, use a genuinely structured form, or cut the unit. |
| Portability | Could this wording travel unchanged and still appear complete? | Check whether this claim, evidence, and reader need selected the wording. If they did, keep it; otherwise rewrite or cut. |

Judge moves at density, never isolated tokens: one familiar phrase is not the failure; a unit where padding, parallelism, vague authority, inflated significance, and unearned hedging cluster together is. Keep the three bins separate: register vices are quality judgments about text, provenance artifacts are evidence about origin, and fabrications are integrity failures. Do not infer authorship from prose quality, and do not reduce an integrity failure to style.

### Step 1: Run the Legitimate-Twin Test

Every suspect form has legitimate uses. Ask:

> Does this form do work a plainer form cannot?

- A separator that prevents a syntactic misread can earn its place.
- Three literal items can earn a three-item list.
- A hedge calibrated to verification reach can earn its place.
- A form used only for rhythm, padding, performed balance, or simulated voice does not.

If the form has a legitimate function, keep it and, if needed, defend that specific use in one line. The defense is per instance, not permission for a habit. If the same use is flagged again, restructure rather than repeating the defense. If the operator directs a specific replacement, execute it; the within-family restriction governs the agent's self-chosen substitutions, not explicit editing instructions. Inventing a function only to preserve a form is the heuristic failing one level up.

### Step 2: Name the Move

| Form | Move it may perform | Repair above the form |
|---|---|---|
| Clause connector | Connective tissue for a relationship never built | Build the relationship or cut the second clause. |
| Summary signpost | Coherence for an argument that never concluded | Reach the conclusion or remove the reaching sentence. |
| Padded list | Cadence around one point | State the point once. |
| Pseudo-empathetic opener | Politeness in place of directness | Deliver the direct statement. |
| Repeated label opener | Voice for a status that asserts nothing | State what changed and what is needed. |
| Fragment stack | Simulated urgency or voice | Write a sentence from the position or delete the unit. |
| Copula avoidance | Symmetry padding that avoids saying what something is | Say what it is. |
| Vague authority | Transfer of claim ownership to an unnamed source | Own the claim or cite the actual source. |

### Step 3: Restructure or Cut

A rhetorical form has two repair outcomes:

1. Restructure the unit around its position.
2. Cut the unit because it has no position worth preserving.

Do not trade within a rhetorical family:

| Family | Common substitutions |
|---|---|
| Clause connection | dash, semicolon, colon-as-connector, parenthetical aside, stacked short sentences |
| Turn scaffolding | summary labels, colon-label openers, repeated bold labels |
| Enumeration cadence | triplets, four-part replacements, inline lettered lists, comma-spliced runs |
| Tone hedges | "might," "appears to," "arguably," "I believe" used only to soften |
| Portable emphasis | one stock importance word exchanged for a near-synonym |

Cross-family substitutions can launder the same move too. The family table is a correction log, not an exhaustive taxonomy: it makes common repeats easy to see, while the named move catches the rest.

### Step 4: Diff the Claims

After the rewrite, compare claims, not just style. For each original claim:

- Is it still asserted?
- Is its strength the same?
- Is the owner the same?

A change is legitimate when the repair states why: re-examining the evidence justified a downgrade, new evidence arrived, or the operator directed the change. An unexplained shift from a definite to a maybe, or from an owned claim to "some observers say," is laundering at the commitment layer.

### Step 5: Ship the Repair

Before delivery, audit the repair against the named move and family table, including cross-family substitutions. Then return the repaired artifact, not a compliance summary. Brief disclosures are appropriate when a form was deliberately retained, a unit was cut, or a claim was recalibrated. If the disclosures become longer than the repair, the narration has taken over again.

This rule governs replies to corrections. Change records, release notes, and requested audits may legitimately describe what changed because that account is their content.

## Negotiation

| Parameter | Default | Adjust when |
|---|---|---|
| Form classification | The legitimate-twin test decides; one defense per instance | A more specific genre rule or explicit operator edit controls. |
| Editing authority | Repair agent-produced prose directly; analyze or rewrite supplied operator prose only when requested or required to fulfill the task | Formal structures may be denser when the structure itself carries content. |
| Hedge species | Preserve evidence-calibrating hedges; remove politeness hedges; verify, remove, or explicitly scope evasive hedges | Named non-verification is calibration only when it states what verification requires and why that work is currently out of reach. If verification is cheap, perform it. When uncertain between calibration and politeness, preserve until evidence supports removal. |
| Claim changes | Preserve strength and ownership by default | Recalibrate when re-examined evidence, new evidence, or explicit direction warrants it. |

## Why This Works

Surface substitution feels like progress because each revision is visibly different. The rhetorical category can remain unchanged across every revision. Naming the move exposes the stable category, just as Category Lock-In exposes a stable solution category beneath varied proposals.

The method is externally checkable. A reviewer holding both versions can see whether the unit was restructured, whether the substitution stayed inside a family, and whether claims drifted. This is stronger than relying on the agent to report that it "applied the principle."

The positive direction matters. Negative-only token rules tell the model where not to step but not where to go. "Name the move, repair above the form, preserve claims or explain their change" supplies a destination.

## When NOT to Apply

- Forms that pass the legitimate-twin test. The test binds the reviewer too; token prosecution is not an audit.
- Provenance artifacts and fabrications, which belong to provenance and verification analysis rather than style repair.
- Operator-requested audits, walkthroughs, or review reports.
- Operator-authored prose, unless analysis or revision was requested or is necessary to fulfill the task. Authorship does not decide quality.
- Hedges that accurately mark verification reach.
- Minor-edit requests that do not flag a tell.
- Operator-requested evidence dumps and process-display artifacts: a research log, exploration trail, or raw deliverable the operator requested. The request selected the pile; reader need selected the wording.
- Requested-neutral artifacts: facilitation, mediation, or options-only menus owe no verdict; do not inject a position the operator did not request. Disguised recommendations are Loaded Comparison or Framed Menu territory.

## Relationship to Pillars

The register frame is carried here: position selects words, quality judgments concern text rather than authorship, and density matters more than isolated tokens. **Committed Contribution** governs claim posture; this heuristic governs the artifact that carries the claim. **Epistemological Grounding**, **External Verification**, and **Verification Reach** govern fabricated or unsupported claims even when the prose is register-clean. **Grounding Preflight** catches coherence simulating evidence at the reasoning layer; this heuristic catches register coherence simulating content at the prose layer. The same fill mechanism appears outside prose in code shape, plans, tool sequences, and scaffolds; that wider channel is a pattern-synthesis trigger under the corpus's derivation method.

**Category Lock-In** is the sibling at proposal altitude. Both catch fluent iteration inside an unnamed category.

**Corrected-Assumption Leakage** audits downstream reasoning after a factual correction. Tell Laundering audits the corrected unit itself for a near-neighbor substitution.

**Frame Abandonment** also treats prior output as evidence of a broken model rather than structure that must be preserved.

The broader letter-for-work substitution can appear outside prose in rename-only refactors, verification theater, test theater, and plan compression. Those channels remain a pattern-synthesis trigger; this heuristic stays scoped to prose repair and self-review.

## Summary

Route the flag, run the register core (Position and Portability), run the legitimate-twin test, name the move, restructure or cut without trading inside a family, compare the claims for unexplained drift, and ship the repair instead of narrating it.
