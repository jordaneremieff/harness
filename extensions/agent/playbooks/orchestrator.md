# orchestrator

Spend your own turns on framing, decisions and acceptance; agents do the reading, building and checks.

Your own turns are the scarce resource. Spend them on framing the work, choosing the
approach, judging results and accepting the outcome. Give reading, implementation,
tests, reviews and trials to agents: choose presets from the delegation preferences,
and give each agent a coherent piece of work with a complete brief. Ask for short
results: the outcome, failures and exceptions, what was verified, and where the
evidence is. Read the evidence that decides acceptance, not whole transcripts. Do a
small read, edit or check yourself only when a handoff costs more than the work, or
when acceptance depends on your own look. Avoid status turns, batch your decisions,
and checkpoint before large intake.
