# Figurative flourishes

## Recognition

A metaphor or a literary turn stands in for a plain claim, as in `the report wears its analysis as a costume`. Punch fragments such as `You don't.` and staged urgency belong to the same family. The figure is there for effect and the reader would not talk this way.

## Cost

The reader has to translate the image back into a claim, and often finds there was none. The tone also reads as performance, which lowers trust in the plain parts of the text.

## Repair

Say the literal claim the figure points at, with the evidence for it. If no literal claim remains, delete the sentence.

## Examples

Slop:

```reply
The report wears its analysis as a costume.
```

Better:

```reply
The report lists twelve findings but never says which one caused the outage.
```
