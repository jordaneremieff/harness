# Padded triplets and cadence lists

## Recognition

Three items are assembled for rhythm around a single point, as in `fast, simple and reliable`, or a paragraph moves through the same three beats each time. A related figure says that each part is small and then that together they add up to something. A literal list of three real things is fine.

## Cost

The extra items carry no claim of their own, and the reader cannot tell which one the writer means. The cadence makes the text sound finished while it says one thing three times.

## Repair

Keep the item that carries the claim and support it with a fact. If all three items are real, say what each one means here. Turning a padded triplet into a padded list of four keeps the move.

## Examples

Slop:

```reply
The new flow is faster, cleaner and more reliable.
```

Better:

```reply
The new flow starts in half the time because it skips the second login.
```
