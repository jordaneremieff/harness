# Essay replies to short questions

## Recognition

A short question gets a long, structured reply with headings, categories, a thesis and a closing summary. The reader has to scroll, zoom out or paste the reply elsewhere to finish it. After a complaint about length, the next reply swings to one dense block that is just as hard to read.

## Cost

The answer the reader wanted sits somewhere inside text they did not ask for. Length that the content does not need costs the reader time on every reply and trains them to skim.

## Repair

Answer a short question in two or three plain sentences that lead with the answer. Add detail only when the reader needs it to act or to check the claim, and put long material in a document instead of the chat.

## Examples

Slop:

```reply
## Summary

There are three things to understand here.

## 1. Background

The scheduler was built to ...

## 2. The root cause

Underneath all of it is one mistake ...

## 3. What this means

In short, ...
```

Better:

```reply
The job ran twice because the scheduler and the hook both started it. I removed the hook, so it now runs once a day.
```
