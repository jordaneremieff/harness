# Heuristic: Governing Context

## Recognition

This heuristic fires when deep understanding has been decomposed into tasks, specifications, handovers, or delegated units and the originating context is about to be discarded.

It also fires when independently originated efforts become interdependent: an output or changed commitment from one effort changes the next decision in another. The efforts need the governing frame for that dependency, not an invented common objective.

Cues:

- The units capture what to do but not how to judge their outputs.
- Several tasks arose from one first-principles exploration and are interdependent.
- Outputs are not yet known, so success cannot be fully encoded as local acceptance criteria.
- The originating reasoning contains rejected alternatives or conditional judgments absent from the task text.
- A fresh worker could execute each unit but could not integrate the results.
- An independently originated effort needs restrictions, relevant state, or acceptance conditions that another effort holds.
- A stated commitment changes while another effort still relies on it.

## Move

Before discarding the originating understanding, or making a decision that depends on another effort, test the relevant artifacts and exchange for four properties:

1. **Task specification:** what to do.
2. **Evaluation criteria:** how to tell whether the result is good.
3. **Dependency awareness:** how it relates to other units from the same decomposition or to independently originated efforts.
4. **Output handling:** who or what consumes the result and which decision follows.

If any of properties 2 through 4 is missing:

- preserve a reachable orchestration point; or
- write a governing-context artifact containing the shared frame, dependencies, rejected alternatives, ensemble success criteria, and integration plan.

For independently originated efforts, exchange only the frame needed for the dependent decision: purpose, applicable restrictions and their sources, relevant state, the dependency, the next consequential act, and who is responsible for it. Preserve each effort's independent scope; an existing reachable exchange can hold this frame. A direct exchange is sufficient when the frame remains available to the parties who need it; no particular shared medium is required.

Publish a changed commitment or actionable result to the party whose next step depends on it, through the agreed reachable surface. State what no longer holds and what remains pending. Seek a revised agreement only where the change invalidates the prior arrangement; silence is not acceptance. Where a dependent action relies on agreement, ensure the proposal and response reach the affected parties; a local acknowledgment is not a shared agreement.

If a published plan remains a discovery source for other efforts, update it or mark it complete when it changes or ends. A private completion message does not refresh that source.

Then run a fresh-reader test:

> Could someone with only these artifacts judge the outputs and make the dependent integration decisions without reconstructing the originating conversations?

If not, the decomposition or dependent exchange is not yet self-sufficient.

## Negotiation

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| Tasks are independent and outputs have local tests | No separate governing artifact is needed. |
| The frame is simple and fully captured in each task | Avoid duplication. |
| Work is sequenced or outputs alter later tasks | Preserve dependencies and decision points explicitly. |
| Independently originated efforts become interdependent | Exchange the minimum frame for the dependent decision without replacing their distinct purposes. |
| A commitment changes or a result becomes available | Update the affected party through the reachable surface; do not duplicate a reliable update that already carries the needed information. |
| Exploration rejected plausible alternatives | Preserve why they were rejected and what evidence would reopen them. |
| Originating context cannot persist reliably | Extract the governing frame before handoff. |
| Sensitive evidence shaped the frame | Store raw evidence privately and expose only the transferable criteria needed by the receiver. |

## Why This Works

Decomposition preserves components more readily than composition. A task description can be locally complete while the set is globally incomplete because no unit owns the judgment that connects outputs.

Unknown outputs make this especially important. Their evaluator needs a framework for interpreting surprises, trading one result against another, and deciding what happens next. Conclusions record what was chosen; derivation often records why, why not alternatives, and what would change the choice.

Independent efforts can also reach a shared decision with different local context. The minimum exchange makes the dependency interpretable; a changed commitment needs to reach the effort that relies on it.

The heuristic does not require preserving an entire conversation. It requires preserving the minimal frame that makes outputs and dependent decisions governable.

## When NOT to Apply

- Tasks are genuinely independent.
- Outputs and the decisions that consume them are fully specifiable locally.
- The governing frame has already been captured, linked, and remains current for the dependent decision.
- The work follows a stable routine whose integration rules are already documented.
- Preserving more context would add noise without changing evaluation.

## Relationship to Pillars

- **Cognitive Stratification:** governs extraction from transient or working context into durable handoff state.
- **Compositional Simplicity:** the composition layer is part of the system and cannot be deleted merely to make tasks look self-contained.
- **Committed Contribution:** the decomposition embodies claims about what matters and should make those criteria inspectable.
- **Phantom Stewardship:** a durable governing frame keeps unfinished substrate from becoming anonymous residue.
- **[Concurrent Work Discovery](heuristic-concurrent-work-discovery.md):** reveals relevant concurrent activity; this heuristic preserves the frame when the efforts are interdependent.
- **[Message Role Mapping](pattern-message-role-mapping.md):** preserves the scope and authority of carried restrictions and commitments within the exchanged frame.
- **Redundant Corroboration:** sibling outputs sharing one frame should not be mistaken for independent facets.

## Summary

Decomposed tasks capture actions. Preserve how outputs are judged, related, and integrated before discarding the originating understanding. When independent efforts become interdependent, exchange the minimum governing frame and publish changed commitments to the affected party.
