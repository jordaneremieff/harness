# Principle: Unearned Prose

## Statement

Prose must carry a position that selects its wording and its place. When the register, a template, available material, or render style selects instead, the prose is filled from no position.

## Core

The register core applies two tests to the whole unit:

| Test | Question | A failing answer means |
|---|---|---|
| Position | What position does this unit carry, and could it be wrong? | No checkable position. The unit asserts nothing, or it piles checkable facts that supply no judgment or conclusion the artifact owes. Form the position, obtain the missing input, use a genuinely structured form, or cut the unit. |
| Portability | What selected this unit's wording and its place: the claim, the evidence, a reader need, or a task term (request, mandate, convention)? | If one of those did, keep it. If a template slot, available material, a mirrored input, or generic wording that could appear in any artifact provided it instead, the unit is filled from no position: rewrite it to carry something the argument owes, or cut it. |

For the reader-need selector, apply a counterfactual holding the reader's role, misconception, and action need constant: would this wording survive a reader with those same attributes who prefers the opposite conclusion? If not, reader preference selected it, and preference is an anti-selector.

Keep three bins separate: register vices are quality judgments about text; provenance artifacts are evidence about origin; fabrications are integrity failures. Do not infer authorship from prose quality, and do not reduce an integrity failure to style. An anti-selector claim under Portability must name its observable — the slot, the mirrored phrase, or the on-hand pile — just as a provenance claim may not rest on prose quality alone.

Judge at density, never isolated tokens. One familiar phrase is not the failure. A unit where padding, parallelism, vague authority, inflated significance, and unearned hedging cluster is.

## Rationale

Prose written from a position is checkable: a reader can ask what it asserts and whether it could be wrong, and the answer selects the wording. Prose written from no position has nothing to check, so the space fills with what is available: the register's familiar machinery, a template's slots, material that happened to be on hand, or render style reaching for cadence. The result can read clean while asserting nothing the artifact owes.

Two moments produce positionless prose. At draft time a unit is filled from no position by slot filling, evidence dumps, register lexicon at density, or unprompted position overwrite. At repair time a flagged tell is answered by substitution: the register overwrites a position that was already present, satisfying the letter of a check while avoiding the work. The two share a mechanism — the register selecting where a position should — but the draft-time form is unprompted and the repair-time form is a response to a flag. This principle owns the frame that recognizes both; the repair procedure is Tell Laundering.

A position is not certainty. It is a checkable assertion a reader can inspect, disagree with, and correct. Positionless prose cannot be corrected because it asserts nothing to correct.

## Shape

```text
POSITION selects -> wording + presence
   |
   v
register-clean unit: checkable, claim-selected, dense only where earned

failure: something else selects
   - register (familiar machinery) -> slot fill
   - template (scaffold slot)      -> section exists because the outline had it
   - available material            -> content present because it was on hand
   - render style                  -> sentence exists for cadence, not content
```

The frame's axioms: position selects words; quality judgments concern the text, not the author; density matters more than isolated tokens.

## Manifestations

### Pattern: Position-Selected Prose

A unit that asserts something checkable, whose wording the claim and reader need selected, earns its place. A reader can state what it claims and what would overturn it.

### Anti-Pattern: Fill From No Position

A section exists because the template had a heading. It asserts something checkable in task-specific wording, but the argument does not need it. It passes a token check and contributes nothing the artifact owes. The scaffold did the selecting.

### Anti-Pattern: Register Overwrite

A position was present and a revision replaced it with register machinery: a hedge where there was a claim, a summary label where there was a conclusion, vague authority where there was ownership. The prose reads cleaner and asserts less.

### Anti-Pattern: Render-Style Selection

A sentence exists for cadence or parallelism rather than content. One familiar phrase is not the failure. The failure is a unit where such forms cluster.

## Tensions

### Position vs. Structured Form

Some units legitimately carry no prose position: a table, a code block, a diagram, a genuinely structured form whose structure is the content, a mandated genre section, or reader-serving scaffolding such as orienting transitions. Position is required of prose units that owe the argument a claim, not of every component; reader need and convention are valid selectors under Portability.

### Position vs. In-Progress Drafting

Drafting is often how a position is discovered, and in-progress exploration is legitimately positionless. The register core judges units presented as complete — delivered or submitted for review — not the act of drafting. Prosecuting exploration as fill would punish the search for a position.

### Position vs. Requested Pile

An operator-requested evidence dump, research log, or raw deliverable is selected by the request, not by a position. The request selected the pile; reader need selected the wording. This principle does not require such artifacts to argue.

### Position vs. Contribution

Committed Contribution governs whether an agent contributes a checkable claim at all. This principle governs whether the prose carrying that claim is position-selected. The first catches permission seeking; the second catches register fill.

### Register Scope vs. Provenance

A unit that semantically mirrors an input while supplying a genuine owed position, with wording tailored enough to leave no literal trace, is register-clean from the artifact alone. Its mirroring is a provenance fact, detectable only against the input, outside this frame's reader-only scope. The three bins keep that question separate from register quality.

## When NOT to Apply

- Provenance artifacts and fabrications, which belong to provenance and verification rather than register quality.
- Structured components whose structure is the content.
- Requested-neutral artifacts: facilitation, mediation, or options-only menus owe no verdict. Do not inject a position the operator did not request; disguised recommendations are Loaded Comparison or Framed Menu territory.

## Relationship to Other Pillars

- **Tell Laundering:** operationalizes the repair. This principle supplies the register core; Tell Laundering runs the legitimate-twin test, names the move, and restructures or cuts.
- **Committed Contribution:** governs claim posture and ownership; this principle governs the prose that carries the claim.
- **Epistemological Grounding, External Verification, Verification Reach:** govern fabricated or unsupported claims even when the prose is register-clean.
- **Grounding Preflight:** catches coherence simulating evidence at the reasoning layer; this principle catches register coherence simulating content at the prose layer.
- **Category Lock-In:** the sibling at proposal altitude. Both catch fluent variation inside an unnamed category, one in solution proposals, one in prose.

## Summary

Position selects the wording and the place. Prose filled from no position is register fill, not contribution.
