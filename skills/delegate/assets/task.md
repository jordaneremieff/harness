# Task contract

## Purpose <!-- required -->
One sentence: why this task exists and where it fits the larger goal.

## Objective <!-- required -->
The single outcome the worker must deliver, stated as an observable end state.

## Operator authority <!-- required -->
The operator's words verbatim in quotes, with the source (session, date, or thread).

## Restrictions and exclusions <!-- required -->
What the worker must not do: paths, acts, tools, or decisions reserved to others.

## Permitted edits and acts <!-- required -->
The exact paths, branches, and acts this task authorises.

## Starting sources
Files, threads, notes, or URLs to read first, with why each matters.

## Skills to load
The skills the worker reads before work, with the reason, such as dig for an evidence question; leave empty when this contract carries the whole method.

## Dependencies and other owners
Work this task depends on or must not touch, and who owns that work.

## Acceptance evidence <!-- required -->
The checks, outputs, or observable facts that prove the objective is met.

## Output format and length <!-- required -->
The shape and size of the final answer: one message, its sections, its length bound.

## End condition <!-- required -->
The state at which the worker stops and reports, including the blocker form. Post to threads and make other tool calls first; the last message is the complete deliverable.

## Reply route <!-- required -->
The exact session identity that receives the answer, and any thread to update.

## Thread ID
The collaboration thread to join or read, when the work is shared.

## Provider or budget notes
Quota walls, substitution order, or budget limits the worker must respect.
