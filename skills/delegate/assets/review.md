# Review contract

## Claim under review <!-- required -->
The exact claim, change, or artifact the reviewer must judge.

## Evidence required <!-- required -->
The evidence each verdict must reach: primary sources, tool definitions, or test results.

## Independence <!-- required -->
Direct the reviewer to verify from primary sources and not adopt the author's conclusion.

## Context
Verified background the reviewer needs, each fact with its source.

## Restrictions and exclusions <!-- required -->
What the reviewer must not do: edits, publication, or scope beyond the claim.

## Output format and length <!-- required -->
Verdict form: each finding with its disposition and cited evidence; the length bound.

## End condition <!-- required -->
When the review stops: every finding dispositioned, or the exact blocker named. Post to threads and make other tool calls first; the last message is the complete deliverable.

## Reply route <!-- required -->
The exact session identity that receives the verdict. For thread posts, keep contribution within 256 characters and message within 8,000 characters; write longer content to a file and post its path.

## Provider or budget notes
Quota walls or budget limits the reviewer must respect.
