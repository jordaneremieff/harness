# Thread frame

## Title <!-- required -->
A short thread title; the agent_collaborate title field.

## Purpose <!-- required -->
The shared purpose and use path; the agent_collaborate purpose field.

## Authority <!-- required -->
The operator's decision verbatim in quotes; the agent_collaborate authority field.

## Source <!-- required -->
Where the authority was given: session, date, or thread; the agent_collaborate source field.

## Restrictions <!-- required -->
The boundaries that bind every member; the agent_collaborate restrictions field.

## Acceptance <!-- required -->
How the thread's work is judged complete; the agent_collaborate acceptance field.

## Integrator
The session identity that integrates results; omit to default to the creating session.
