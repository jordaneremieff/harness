## Essay shape

A short answer built like an essay: categories, a thesis and a closing state, when the reader asked for one fact or decision.

Repair direction: Lead with the answer. Cut the framing and the closing state.

Handled by: Procedure step 1: write the position first.

## Paragraph breaks without a change of thought

Paragraph breaks that mark rhythm or emphasis instead of a real change of thought.

Repair direction: Merge paragraphs that make one point. Break only where the point changes.

Handled by: Procedure step 4: repair above the form.

## The reader's words returned with polish

The reply restates the reader's own request in smoother words and asserts nothing new.

Repair direction: State what you found, decided or need. Drop the restatement.

Handled by: Procedure step 1: write the position first.

## Units that assert nothing checkable

Sentences that nothing could contradict, and true facts that supply no judgement.

Repair direction: Add the claim, the number or the check. Cut the sentence if it has none.

Handled by: Procedure step 1: write the position first.

## Evidence without a conclusion

Lists of facts, outputs or findings with no statement of what they mean or what to do.

Repair direction: Put the conclusion first, then keep only the evidence that supports it.

Handled by: Procedure step 1: write the position first.

## Self-review that clears a token checklist

A review that checks banned words off a list and passes a draft whose structure and claims are unchanged.

Repair direction: Re-anchor on structure and claims. A token-free draft can still carry the move.

Handled by: Procedure step 4: repair above the form, and references/repair.md.

## Clipped rewrite without a tighter argument

A rewrite that is shorter and choppier but makes the same loose argument.

Repair direction: Rebuild from the claim and the evidence. Shorter is not tighter.

Handled by: Procedure step 4: repair above the form.

## Claims drifting weaker or ownerless

A style repair that dilutes a definite claim, adds hedges or moves ownership to a passive or an unnamed source.

Repair direction: Run the claim diff. A weaker claim needs a stated reason.

Handled by: Procedure step 5 and the claim diff table in references/repair.md.

## Self-review treated as exempt

A review section that is written to a lower standard than the text it reviews.

Repair direction: Hold the review to the same standard as the deliverable.

Handled by: Procedure step 4: repair above the form.

## Recognising a failure inside a message that repeats it

A message that names its own fault and commits the same fault in the same breath.

Repair direction: Fix the failure in the text. Do not describe it.

Handled by: Procedure step 4: repair above the form.

## Writing for a blank reader and replaying the session

Either assumes the reader saw everything, or replays the whole session to a reader who needs only the result.

Repair direction: Write for a reader who arrives cold and give only what changes their next move.

Handled by: Procedure step 2: draft for a reader who arrives cold.

## Menus built on context the reader lacks

Options and decisions that use names and states the reader never saw.

Repair direction: Recommend one option, say why, and give the missing context in two or three sentences.

Handled by: Procedure step 2: draft for a reader who arrives cold.

## Restoring context indiscriminately

Adding all background to fix a missing-context flag, so the reply grows without helping.

Repair direction: Add only the context the next decision needs.

Handled by: Procedure step 2: draft for a reader who arrives cold.

## Length that forces scrolling

A reply longer than the reader's task needs, in one pass on the screen.

Repair direction: Cut units the reader is not owed. Prefer deleting whole paragraphs.

Handled by: Procedure step 2: draft for a reader who arrives cold.

## A local edit that drops a settled requirement

A fix to one passage that silently removes a requirement agreed earlier.

Repair direction: Diff the requirements and claims against the earlier draft after each edit.

Handled by: Procedure step 5: diff your claims.

## A corrected assumption treated as a wording patch

An assumption is fixed in one sentence while it still holds up other parts of the text.

Repair direction: Search the whole text for what depends on the assumption and revise each part.

Handled by: Procedure step 5: diff your claims.

## Project terms defined at first use

A project or invented term used without a definition. The meter flags only unexplained code-like tokens.

Repair direction: Define the term in ordinary words at first use, or use an ordinary word.

Handled by: Procedure step 2: draft for a reader who arrives cold.
